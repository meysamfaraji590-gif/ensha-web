**The Short Route**

- It is shorter and gets you to the destination sooner.
- The smell of sweet pastries baking in the bakery next to the street.
- The sounds of people and shopkeepers can be heard.
- Some parts are crowded.
- The street is paved with cobblestones.
- At night, the streetlights turn on.

**The Long Route**

- It is longer and takes more time to reach the destination.
- The smell of soil and grass after the rain.
- The sound of the river and sometimes birds singing can be heard.
- After the rain, some parts of the path become muddy.
- It passes alongside a river.
- Tall trees stand on both sides of the path.
- There is a small wooden bridge along the way.
- At one point, you can see a wide view of the surrounding area.

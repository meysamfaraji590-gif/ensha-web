### One Sentence, Three Versions

Choose one of the following sentences:

**1.** The door opened, and someone walked in.

**2.** The boy looked under the bed.

**3.** Someone was standing behind the window.

**4.** The cat jumped down from the table.

**5.** When they opened the box, there was something inside.

Now rewrite your chosen sentence three times:

- Once in a **funny** way
- Once in a **scary** way
- Once in a **strange and imaginative** way

Keep the main event in the original sentence, but change the words and details to give each version a different mood.
